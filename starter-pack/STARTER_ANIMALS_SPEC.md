# SURVIVAL JOURNEY — STARTER ANIMALS SPEC v1.0
2026-10-24 | Authority: 7 uploaded starter concept boards, not measured mesh data.

## Global anatomical / topology contract
Coordinate Y up; +Z forward; meters; front facing +Z. Root at ground contact. Each preset supplies reference height, ratio-based body shapes, component specs, landmark positions, palette, LOD targets, animation sockets. All numerical ratios are proposed rebuild targets inferred visually from perspective/reference sheets, **not measured vertex values**. Shared triangular facet grammar: broad planes on torso, higher facet density at face/joints, outward-overlapping tapered triangles for fur; avoid high-frequency white-noise geometry. Mirror left/right major anatomy but intentionally vary tuft angles 4–15°. Preserve topology through the animation ranges. For each animal emit geometry, material, rig and export manifest. A static concept wireframe does not prove usable rig/skinning.
Reference screens contain multiple panels. Render / validate 3/4 shaded, 3/4 wire, side wire, front wire (where present) with common ground line and height; maintain screenshot provenance.

## Individual body part specification
### WOLF — starter_wolf_v1
Image: wolf board with 3/4 shaded, 3/4 wire, side wire, front wire.
Unit S=shoulder height. Body snout to tail-base length 1.35–1.55 S; rump at .88–1.0 S. Skull length .40–.50 S, width .25–.34 S. Muzzle length 0.20–0.27 S (wedge taper toward dark black nose); bridge should stay planar; eye at .54–.63 of muzzle-to-occiput projection. Ears two tall triangular prisms .14–.18 S above head, slight outward tilt; asym ear edges. Cheek mane 3–5 layer rows tapering into chest; visible white ventral bib, deep charcoal dorsal mane and slate main coat. Shoulder foreleg joins chest with 2–3 cross-section transitions; carpal joint bends forward/back cleanly. Hindquarters have angled upper leg and prominent hock. Paws wide, 3–4 toe wedges, claws separate dark tiny tips. Tail .45–.60 S with coarse angular fur rings and point. Required part sockets: root, spine, neck, jaw_optional, tail_01..03, hip L/R, shoulder L/R, ear L/R, paw L/R front/back. 4 material IDs; suggested LOD0 1200–2200 triangles. Pattern: faceted coat, no fur cards texture.
### STAG — starter_stag_v1
Image: stag board, 3/4 shaded + 3/4/side/front wire.
S=shoulder height. Nose-to-rump body length 1.10–1.30S. Skull .38–.46S, muzzle narrow tapered wedge, eyes positioned lateral. Long neck rises to head 1.45–1.65S. Chest wedge and pale beard bib triangular chest tufts. Tall, thin tapering forelegs with distinct carpal, ankle and split black hooves; rear leg contains visible hock setback. Large laterally spreading antlers dominate outline: branch bases from paired frontal skull pedicles; 4–6 tines each side as proposed target, decreasing tube radius from .045–.065 S base to .008–.014 S tips, organic centerline, shallow plane facet bands; max rack width ~0.9–1.5 S. Antler top extends .45–.70 S above skull; must not intersect ears/head. Chest/rump warm brown, neck/throat cream, dark hoof. Separate part groups: head, ears L/R, antlers L/R, torso, chest tuft, 4 legs, hoof pairs, tail. Rig sockets for separate antler shed/growth and neck yaw. LOD0 1600–2800 triangles.
### DOE — starter_doe_v1
Image: doe spotted body board, 3/4 shaded + 3/4/side/front wire.
Shares anatomical *skeleton family* with stag, not identical silhouette. S=shoulder height. Body 1.08–1.24 S. Slim neck, narrow chest with soft angular barrel and raised hip volume. Head .38–.46S; muzzle longer/slimmer than rabbit, short black nose patch. Ears conspicuously tall .18–.26S, oriented outward 25–55 degrees with dark rim and peach inner shell. Eyes large low-detailed almond disks. No antlers. Legs slender with narrow ankles, pointed cloven hooves; rear hock visible. Tiny drooping tail with pale underside. Dorsal back white spots in sparse irregular staggered groups (suggest 8–25 patches), coherent shapes *not* random confetti; spots may be vertex colors or low-poly conforming panels, preserve palette. LOD0 1000–1800.
### SABERTOOTH — starter_sabertooth_v1
Image: sabertooth cat board, 3/4 roaring shaded + 3/4/side/front wire.
S=shoulder height. Hull elongated 1.35–1.60S nose to rump. Broad powerful chest width .56–.75S, shoulders fuller than hip. Skull massive .50–.62S, high forehead, cheek ruff in 3–5 triangular overlapping layers, pale lower chin and chest. Jaw must have articulated lower section with black-red inner mouth and separate upper/lower teeth. Two giant tusk-like upper canines sweep downward .18–.28S beyond upper lip, gradually curved/tapered; mouth-open rest variant must not be baked into head silhouette for all animation states. Triangular ears, angular nose and eyebrows emphasize predator. Forelegs large with big paws 4 toes; rear legs slightly slimmer and high tarsus. Tail .40–.55S, taper with dark tip. Contrasting amber brown and creamy chest. Rig nodes jaw, saber_L/R, mane shells, tail01..03. LOD0 1500–2500 triangles.
### MAMMOTH ADULT — starter_mammoth_adult_v1
Image: upper/lower 4-panel adult mammoth board (two shaded variants), multiple wire poses.
Normalize L = shoulder-to-rump body length. Target adult game model body height 2.8–3.6m (design preset). Back crest height .70–.85L; high domed hump directly over forequarters, sloping back towards hips. Huge trunk originates between eyes, travels anterior-down before curling inward/up; segmented tapered rings 8–14 with radius grading and flexible skeleton chain 5–8 bones. Two large tusks originate beside trunk and curve anterior-lateral-up; use 10–18 centerline rings with 6–9 radial facets; thick ivory root, sharp tapered ends, must avoid trunk intersection and terrain. Ears fan broadly but smaller relative to cranium than modern elephant. Large shaggy fringe strips on crown, cheeks, chest, belly, knees and rump: 3–5 clump length bands, separate angular overlay shells (no random noise spikes). Limbs nearly vertical, thick tapered columns, clear knee breaks and 4–5 broad ivory toenails. Body dark burnt umber with caramel clumps, tusks ivory. Short tassel tail. LOD0 2200–4000 triangles. Required sockets skull, trunk_01..08, tusk L/R, leg 4, tail.
### RABBIT — starter_rabbit_v1
Image: four-panel small rabbit board.
Normalize sitting torso nose-to-rump L. Rounded compact faceted hindquarters; broad rump and short forechest. Sitting height .55–.70L not counting ears. Head-to-body ratio large with generous cheeks, eyes dark .06–.10 head width (proposed), small rose muzzle/nose, 3–5 whisker strokes optional near LOD0 only. Two extremely tall upright but tilted leaf-prism ears each .55–.80L; inner contrasting pale pink. Front short paws tucked under chest; back crouched hindlegs thickly folded and broad hindfeet forward. Pompom tail spherical/faceted .12–.18L. Top copper brown, throat cream, ventral cream. Ear rig base separate from head to animate alertness. LOD0 700–1400 triangles.
### MAMMOTH CALF — starter_mammoth_calf_v1
Image: four-panel calf mammoth reference; cute juvenile proportion.
Use mammoth adult core family but **never uniform-scale adult**. Proportional head +10–18%, eyes +15–25%, visible trunk shorter/thicker and more playful upward curl, legs -10–18% length, stout/wide feet. Tusks 35–55% shorter than adult proportion, still curving outward; ear lateral broad relative to skull. Forehead cap/tufts larger; fluff stacks rounder but faceted, back less domed than adult. Body honey/ginger instead of adult dark umber, cream ear interior and toenails. Separate skull/trunk/tusks/tufts for age morph/animation. Proposed calf body height 1.2–1.7m. LOD0 1500–2600 triangles.

## Mandatory QA gates (all species)
A1: 3D artist and generator use same part names, axes, normalized dimensions and authored seeds.
A2: Reference screenshot panel-by-panel contour checklist (head, ears, muzzle, chest, torso, limbs, tail; plus species-specific antler/tusk/mane).
A3: All solids finite, triangles nondegenerate, watertight where intended; separate alpha cards explicitly allowed for whiskers/fur.
A4: All four paws/hooves groundable, no floating/terrain clipping, correct walking cycle contact.
A5: Species silhouettes remain legible at 64–128 px height and 1:1 game isometric zoom.
A6: LOD1 <=50% triangles of LOD0, LOD2 <=25%, essential species feature always preserved.
A7: Root and per-part bounding boxes, measured triangle/material/draw count exported.
A8: Rig motions (walk/idle/run/eat/attack where appropriate) do not tear layered shells or antler/tusk intersections.
